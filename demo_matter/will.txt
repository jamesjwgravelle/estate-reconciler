LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT OF MARGARET ELLEN HARTWELL
Date: 2019-06-03

I appoint my niece, Diane Colby, as Executor and Trustee of my Estate.
I give the sum of $10,000 to the Kingston Public Library Foundation.
I authorize my Executor to employ a trust company as agent to assist in administering my Estate, and to pay its fees from my Estate.
I direct that the residue of my Estate be divided equally among my grandchildren.
